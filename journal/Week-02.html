Paris Climate normals

The question

How does the seasonal rainful at Paris related to its monthly temperature?

The data

World Historical Climate-Monthly Average for GHCN-Daily stations for 1981-2010 (published by Esri from NOAA NCEI data (GHCN-Daily v3.24, Menne et al. 2012) https://services.arcgis.com/P3ePLMYs2RVChkJx/arcgis/rest/services/World_GHCND_Mly_Clim_Means_1981_2010/FeatureServer/0 Opened by Julie at 09/20/2026

The method

Frist, open FILTER click Add new, operator STARTS WITH FR, then click Add new a second time, field GHCN-D ID, operator is, value FR000007150. Set the operator joining the two expressions to AND. Click Save. With the layer selected, click Configure charts, on the Settings toolbar, then Add chart, then Bar chart. Set Category to Principle Station Name. Set Aggregation to Mean. Under Numeric fields, add all twelve [Month] Avg. Temp C fields, January through December, in order. On the General tab, title it Average monthly temperature, Paris (1981–2010 normal) and label the Y axis Temperature (°C). Add a second bar chart the same way. Category Principle Station Name, aggregation Mean. Numeric fields: the twelve [Month] Mean Prcp mm fields. November is mislabelled; add it anyway, in order. Title it Average monthly precipitation, Paris (1981–2010 normal) and label the Y axis Precipitation (mm).
