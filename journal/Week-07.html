What I found

Paris matters to Frenc export, but France's trade cannot be reduced to Paris. A Connection that holds: Paris's aviation infrastructure and international trade Paris-charles de Gaulle Airport is a major international passenger and air freight hub. In 2024, it handled approximately 1/9 million tones of greight and mail (From governement official data). This demonstrates Paris's rol in connecting France to international markets. Paris transport infrastructure facilitates the movement of goods across borders. However, the airport's cargo volume does not establish that all, or even most, of France exports pass through Paris. A Connection that does not hold: Paris's physical geography as an explanation for France's export basket Paris's ohysical geography cannot by itself explain the composition of France's national export basket. France exports a diverse range of goods, including aircraft, machinery, vehicles, chemicals, and agriculture products, whose production depends on diifferent combinations of natural resources, technology, capital, and industrial specilizations. Furthermore, production and aircraft delivery activities are distributed across multiple locations in different cities in France and Europe. The connection breaks when national export data are treated as direct evidence of what Pairs produces or exports.
