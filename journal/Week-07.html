What I found

Within central Paris, small scattered patches classified as Trees in 2017 have shifted to Built Area by 2024. I suspect that some remaining tree cover in this dataset belongs to private, restricted-access green space rather than publicly accessible parks, and this land cover layer cannot tell the difference.

What this does not show

Land cover classification is a proxy for accessible urban green space, where Z = spectral signature confusion limits the classifier’s ability to separate open public tree canopy from vegetation on private enclosed sites.
