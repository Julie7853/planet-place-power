What I found

At this Paris site, the ground slopes gently from west toward the east and the Seine River, so rainwater flows eastward toward the Seine during rainfall. Eroded surface material moves down the gentle slope with surface runoff toward the river channel. Most transported materials will eventually settle and deposit on the Seine’s floodplain.

What this does not show

For example, this map and layers can not support the claim that the slope difference between the left and right bank of Seine River (or the maximum difference in slope of the city Paris) is smaller than 3 degrees (27 degrees). Because the data uses a 1m cell size, but slope values are averaged across each grid cell. Tiny, short, steep river banks are smaller than the grid’s averaging window. The map smooths these small sharp slopes and cannot prove this claim. Also the vertical uncertainty is 0.20m, which is enough to create elevation error.
