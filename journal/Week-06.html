Land Use for Now and Then

The question

What was the land of Paris used for before its major urban change, what pattern did it follow, what has it become, and how has the distribution of benefits from this transformation worked out for different groups?

The data

Sentinel-2 10m Land Use/Land Cover Time Series, Esri and Impact Observatory, 1:37795, 1px=10m, 2017-2024(2024 was the Paris Olympic year)2020as the third year because of the start of the pandemic. Esri MLA with the source data CC BY 4.0.

I am using Tree and Built Area cover to stand for accessible urban green space, and it might fail because mixed pixels in dense neighbourhoods force the classifier to pick one dominant class and discard other surface features. I am using X to stand for Y, and it might fail because Z.

What I found

Within central Paris, small scattered patches classified as Trees in 2017 have shifted to Built Area by 2024. I suspect that some remaining tree cover in this dataset belongs to private, restricted-access green space rather than publicly accessible parks, and this land cover layer cannot tell the difference.

What this does not show

Land cover classification is a proxy for accessible urban green space, where Z = spectral signature confusion limits the classifier’s ability to separate open public tree canopy from vegetation on private enclosed sites.
